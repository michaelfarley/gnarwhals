⚑ Once Races Start (practices continue)

The races are now your hard intensity — don't pile hard practice volume on top of racing, that's how kids show up flat.

Normal week: Mon = short quality (a few 2–3 min Hard or 30-sec Sprints) · Thu = genuinely easy recovery spin.

Race week (weekend race): Keep the layout — it already lines up. Mon = quality (5–6 days out, plenty of room to recover), Thu = easy / openers (short, snappy, 1–2 days out), and let the weekend race be the hard effort. Never add hard volume into a race week.

Standing Rules

  • Protect the easy day. Strong, motivated kids turn everything into a race. The Thursday engine day is what makes Monday's intensity actually intense — it is not filler.
  • Intervals run on the clock, not on a feature. Single loop = you can't stage reps on a chosen rise. So YOU call every effort off your watch — "3 min hard, GO" / "30 sec, GO" — and they ride hard from wherever they are. On a descent where hard pedaling is pointless, they keep the effort going as terrain allows and you let it count. Climbs are simply where the hard efforts will naturally bite hardest.
  • Easy climbs are the whole game on the engine day. Z2 dies on MTB climbs. Gear down, spin, full-sentence talk test on every climb. Let descents coast.
  • Gravel ≤ 25% every session. It lives in the warmup/cooldown bookends, where steady effort is easiest to hold anyway.
  • Watch the 13-yr-olds. Wide age range = different recovery. If a younger rider is flat or cranky two sessions running, quietly back them off — no big deal made of it.
  • Your watch audits, the kids' breathing prescribes. If you're in your own Z2 setting climb pace from the front, the wheel behind you is at or below it.
